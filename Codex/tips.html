Global .codexignore

Block heavy, non-essential files from the agent's file reader by configuring ignore lists in your project root[cite: 1]. Lockfiles alone can consume 50,000+ tokens[cite: 1].

  • > Dependencies (node_modules/, vendor/, .venv/)[cite: 1]
  • > Lockfiles (package-lock.json, yarn.lock, pnpm-lock.yaml)[cite: 1]
  • > Build artifacts & maps (dist/, build/, *.map)[cite: 1]
  • > Media & binaries (*.png, *.svg, *.pdf, *.sqlite)[cite: 1]

Terminal Log Piping

Raw build or test failures often produce thousands of lines of redundant output[cite: 1]. Pipe terminal commands through log-pruning tools[cite: 1].

# Filter only error lines and the trailing stack trace
npm test | grep -A 5 -B 2 "FAIL\|Error" | head -n 40[cite: 1]

Delegation & Effort Tuning

Stop using your most expensive model for every query. Pair specific models with strategic effort parameters to balance cost and capability.

  • > The Orchestrator: Use GPT-5.6 Sol to plan architecture or map complex workflows. It requires less "thinking" overhead to perform well.
  • > The Explorer: Embed GPT-5.6 Luna for simple tasks, but force it to xhigh or max effort. The extended reasoning time drastically improves its baseline capability while remaining massively cheaper than Sol.
(i) KNOW YOUR GPT-5.6 MODEL

The GPT-5.6 Codex family operates on a distinct naming convention based on Latin celestial bodies. Selecting the right model and effort combination is critical for token economy.

  • > Sol (Sun): The heavyweight orchestrator. Best for complex, end-to-end engineering tasks. Runs exceptionally well on high or xhigh effort. Very capable, but expensive ($30/1M output tokens).
  • > Terra (Earth): The middle ground. Often recommended to skip in day-to-day workflows, as Sol is more capable and Luna offers similar performance for a fraction of the cost.
  • > Luna (Moon): The fast, cheap explorer. Highly capable for daily coding implementations at an extremely low cost ($1.2/1M output tokens).
